# CS-360 Weight Tracker App

## Project Overview

The Weight Tracker app is an Android app that helps users keep track of their weight and work toward a goal. Users can create an account, log in, add weight entries and update their goal weight. The app also includes an optional text notification to congratulate users when they reach their goal. I wanted to keep the app simple so users could easily track their progress without having to go through a bunch of screens.

## User Interface and Design

The app includes screens for logging in, creating an account, tracking weight and managing SMS notifications. I tried to keep the layout simple and make the main features easy to find. The weight tracker screen includes an Add Weight button and a graph so users can enter their weight and see their progress over time. I also included a goal weight notification to give users a way to recognize their progress. I think keeping things simple makes the app easier to use and helps users focus on their goals.

## Development Approach

I worked on the app one step at a time, starting with the screen layouts and then working on navigation and features. Since I'm still learning how to develop mobile apps, some parts took longer than I expected. I had to figure out how layouts and constraints worked in Android Studio and how to get the screens to work together. Breaking the work into smaller steps helped me work through problems without getting too overwhelmed. I can use the same approach in future projects by planning things out first and testing features as I build them.

## Testing

I used the Android Studio emulator to see how the app looked and check that the screens and buttons worked as expected. I also had to work through build errors and fix layout problems along the way. Testing helped me catch issues that I might not have noticed just by looking at the code. It also showed me how even small mistakes can affect how an app works, so it's important to check things as I go instead of waiting until the end.

## Challenges and Innovation

One of my biggest challenges was figuring out how to turn my UI design into an actual working app. I knew what I wanted the screens to look like, but I didn't always know how to make that happen in Android Studio. I had to try different approaches, troubleshoot problems, and make adjustments along the way. This taught me that developing an app isn't just about writing code. You also have to figure out how everything fits together and make changes when something doesn't work the way you planned.

## Skills Demonstrated

I think one of my biggest strengths in this project was working on the UI design and figuring out how to make the app easy to use. I wanted users to be able to add their weight and check their progress without having to search for those features. Creating the different screens and working on the weight history graph helped me understand how design and code work together. I'm still learning, but this project gave me more experience with Android Studio, Java, XML layouts, and troubleshooting problems.
